Auto-routing

Switch models automatically per build step to cut token spend — mechanical steps go to a free/cheap model, only genuinely hard steps escalate to a stronger paid one. When off, the Manual assignment below is used instead.
Cheap
Handles most steps: renames, boilerplate, small edits. Almost always a free local model.
Mid
Moderately complex steps — multi-file changes, non-trivial logic. Optional.
Strong
Hard steps: refactors, security, concurrency — and any step a cheaper rung got wrong. Optional but recommended.
